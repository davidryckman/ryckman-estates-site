Ask for account-deletion guidance

For an account-deletion question, email ryckmanestates@gmail.com. This public website has no login or account-deletion controls.

What this page confirms

A verified end-to-end app account-deletion procedure is not published on this website. This page is a contact route for questions; it is not a functioning account-deletion service or confirmation that an in-app deletion flow is available.

No completion time, universal-erasure claim or retention exception is promised here. App account deletion and the handling of shared content, media, backups and young members require separate verified instructions.

Before taking action

Deleting an app from your device or signing out does not itself delete an account or server-stored information. Do not treat either action as an account-deletion request.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, invitation tokens, identity documents or private family media in an initial support request.
