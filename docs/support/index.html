Contact support

Email ryckmanestates@gmail.com for help with Ryckman Estates or a question about this website.

For a technical problem, include your device model, operating-system version, app version if relevant, and a short description of what happened. No response-time commitment is made here.

Membership & installation

Ryckman Estates is an invitation-only family space. Visiting this website does not create an app account or grant Estate membership.

This website does not provide a public app download. Contact support if you need guidance about your invitation or installation channel.

Privacy & account questions

For a privacy concern or an account-deletion question, contact ryckmanestates@gmail.com. This website has no sign-in or account-management controls.

Read Website Privacy and Account Deletion for the scope of the information published here.

Share only what is needed

Do not send passwords, verification codes, invitation tokens, identity documents, health information or private family photographs/messages. If a screenshot is needed to explain an issue, remove identifying and sensitive details first.
